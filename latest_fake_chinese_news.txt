HEADLINE: Baidu Deploys New '6G Tera-Link Chipset' in Beijing (Zhongguancun), Achieving ultra-low latency metrics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In a massive technological stride, Baidu has officially introduced its latest innovation, 
the '6G Tera-Link Chipset'. The breakthrough, developed over three years in Beijing (Zhongguancun)'s 
premier R&D facility, aims to revolutionize the industry by achieving ultra-low latency metrics.

Industry experts suggest that this moves China closer to achieving full self-reliance in 
key technological sectors. The development utilizes proprietary architecture and complies 
fully with local industrial standards, positioning Baidu as a major global frontrunner in the Robotics & Telecom space.

Commercial deployment is scheduled to begin late next quarter, with initial rollouts targeting 
infrastructure projects and enterprise ecosystems across key metropolitan regions.
